Import your existing homilies

This reads the six year folders and the FUNERAL HOMILIES folder that sit next to the app, and adds any homily not already in your library. It fills in the cycle year, the liturgical day, and the funeral date and name from the folder and file names, and pulls in the text of each homily. Nothing is thrown away: anything it is unsure about is brought in and marked ⚑ review so you can confirm it by hand. Running it again only adds files that are new.
